# Contributor Notes

Anyone is welcomed to contribute to this project, as long as the contributions are helpful.

## Translating

You can contribute translations by editing [the translation files in the `paiagram` crate](./crates/paiagram/assets/locales/).
The translations are [Fluent trasnlation files](https://projectfluent.org), which should be easy to edit and flexible.
Please don't submit any unreviewed machine/LLM translations. I absolutely hate them.

## AI Policy

We don't encourage or discourage the use of AI/LLM. In general, you may use it to do the following:

- Improve your work.
- Write documentation.

You may not use it to do the following:

- Translate the program to Chinese (Simplified Han Script).
- Write PR/issue description.
- Submit PR/issue/discussion.

AI/LLM is a useful tool, however it must be used by the right person in the right way.
AI submission without human guidance only wastes the maintainer's time. Each submission
should teach both the maintainer and the submitter something new, but if the submitter
doesn't even know what changes are made, it is hard to tell if the sumbission is truely
helpful. You should not submit anything if you don't even understand what the AI is doing.
